❓ Game Rules & Guide

WHO'S THE PROBLEM? is a multiplayer anonymous voting and friendly roasting party game for 3 to 15 players.

🗳️ Voting Rules

• You can vote for any friend in the room, but NEVER for yourself.

• Votes are 100% confidential and anonymous. Individual vote selections are never exposed.

🏆 Scoring System

• +1 Point for receiving the most votes in a normal question (being 'The Problem').

• +2 Points for correctly guessing who receives the most votes in 'Guess the Majority' rounds.

• +2 Points in Random Chaos multiplier rounds!

🎖️ Funny End Game Awards

Based on in-game performance, win titles like Drama King/Queen, Walking Meme, Silent Observer, and more!
